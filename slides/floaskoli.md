  <div style="text-align:center; margin:0.3rem 0;">

      </div>
  sem má leysa fyrir fjölda skoppa:
  <div style="text-align:center; margin-top:0.3rem;">

      <div style="text-align:center; margin:0.3rem 0;">

      <div style="text-align:center; margin:0.3rem 0;">
